**ChocAn**

Chocoholics Anonymous; an organization dedicated to helping people addicted to chocolate in all its glorious forms.



**Member**

A person enrolled in ChocAn program who pays a monthly fee for unlimited consultations and treatments with health care professionals.



**ChocAn Card**

A plastic card with a nine-digit member number encoded into the card that is provided to each member enrolled in ChocAn.



**Provider**

A health care professional, namely, dietitians, internists, and exercise experts, who provides services to ChocAn members and record services through a provider terminal.



**Provider Terminal**

The hardware device, similar to a credit-card terminal, in which a provider uses to validate members and record services.



**ChocAn Data Processing Software**

A data processing software that is responsible for doing key software system operations.



**Provider Directory**

An alphabetically ordered list of service names, service codes and fees which can be sent to the provider as an e-mail attachment on request.



**Service Code**

A six-digit code identifying a specific service, such as a session with a dietitian, or an aerobics exercise session.



**Validated / Invalid Number / Member Suspended**

Status messages returned by the system when a member number has been verified. “Suspended” means the member has not paid membership fees for at least a month.



**Operator**

A ChocAn Data Center staff personnel who can add, delete, and update member and provider records.



**Manager**

A ChocAn supervising staff who can request any weekly report on demand and receives the summary report for accounts payable.



**Acme Accounting Services**

A third-party organization responsible for financial procedures such as recording payments of membership fees, suspending members whose fees are overdue, and reinstating suspended members who have now paid what is owing.



**EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer)**

The mechanism by which providers are paid. In this project, only the data file used for banks are processed and produced, and not the transfer itself (which Acme Accounting Services is responsible for.)


**Actor**

A person or external entity that interacts with the system.



**Use Case**

A description of a way an actor uses the system to accomplish a task.



**<<include>>**

A relationship in which one use case always inserts the behavior of another use case into itself.



**<<extend>>**

A relationship in which one use case is an optional addition or a conditional behavior to a base use case.
